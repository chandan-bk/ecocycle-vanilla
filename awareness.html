What Counts As E-Waste & Why It Matters

Electronics represent only 2% of garbage volume, but 70% of toxic landfill hazards.

✓ What Counts As E-Waste?

  • • IT & Telecom: Laptops, PCs, monitors, smartphones, chargers, cables.
  • • Consumer Electronics: TVs, stereos, gaming consoles, headphones.
  • • Small Appliances: Microwaves, kettles, toasters, power tools.
  • • Power Supplies: Lithium-ion battery packs, power banks, UPS units.

⚠️ Toxic Substances Inside

  • • Lead (Pb): Found in solder and CRT tubes. Known neurotoxin affecting brain health.
  • • Mercury (Hg): In display backlights and switches. Leaches into drinking water.
  • • Cadmium (Cd): In chip resistors and older rechargeable cells. Harms kidney and bone structure.
