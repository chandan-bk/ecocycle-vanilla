What Counts As E-Waste & Why It Matters

Electronics represent only 2% of garbage volume, but 70% of toxic landfill hazards.

What Counts As E-Waste?

  • • IT & Telecom: Laptops, PCs, monitors, smartphones, chargers, cables.
  • • Consumer Electronics: TVs, stereos, gaming consoles, headphones.
  • • Small Appliances: Microwaves, kettles, toasters, power tools.
  • • Power Supplies: Lithium-ion battery packs, power banks, UPS units.

Toxic Substances Inside

  • • Lead (Pb): Found in solder and CRT tubes. Known neurotoxin affecting brain health.
  • • Mercury (Hg): In display backlights and switches. Leaches into drinking water.
  • • Cadmium (Cd): In chip resistors and older rechargeable cells. Harms kidney and bone structure.

FAQ: Why is EcoCycle a Non-Profit Service? Do I Get Paid For My E-Waste?

No, EcoCycle does not purchase devices for cash. Commercial re-commerce websites (like Cashify or OLX) only buy working smartphones that they can resell for profit, leaving behind 80% of hazardous e-waste (burnt cables, smashed displays, swollen batteries, dead appliances).

EcoCycle operates on a civic utility / NGO aggregation model: Citizens surrender obsolete or broken devices for 100% free, and we aggregate these small collections to deliver them in bulk to authorized formal dismantling plants (CPCB/KSPCB certified). This ensures dangerous heavy metals are chemically neutralized rather than poisoned into municipal water or burned in informal open-air scrap yards.
