The Problem: E-Waste Is Piling Up

⚠️

Hazardous Materials

Lead, mercury, cadmium and lithium leach into soil and water tables, causing severe ecosystem damage.

📍

No Easy Way to Recycle

Citizens do not know where verified recycling centres exist, and there is zero doorstep collection logistics.

🙈

Low Awareness

People are unaware of e-waste hazards, safe disposal guidelines, or their personal carbon footprint.

Result: Millions of tonnes of e-waste end up in municipal bins and landfills, not at certified recyclers.

Our Objectives

1
Connect citizens directly with verified recycling centres through interactive GIS mapping
2
Make doorstep e-waste pickup booking frictionless with instant email receipts
3
Quantify environmental impact (CO₂ saved & mass diverted) using empirical models
4
Educate communities about e-waste hazards through interactive literacy quizzes

Database Design (Supabase PostgreSQL)

profiles
id (uuid → auth.users)
email (text)
role ('user' | 'admin')
created_at (timestamptz)
recyclers / centers
id (uuid, pk)
name, city, address
lat, lng (float8)
accepts (text[])
pickups
id (uuid, pk)
user_id → auth.users
item_type, quantity
pickup_date, address, status
Secured with Row Level Security (RLS) + JWT authentication. Joins handled in JS to avoid PGRST200 schema cache errors.

Two Roles, One Unified Portal

👤 Citizen (User)
  • Find certified recyclers on the interactive map
  • Book a scheduled doorstep e-waste collection
  • Track real-time status & view impact metrics
  • Test knowledge with the interactive quiz
🛡️ Admin / Facility Manager
  • Triage and inspect all pickup requests
  • Filter and search pickups by customer email
  • Update pickup status (pending → collected)
  • Add, edit, and delete recycling centres (CRUD)

Secure sign-in via Supabase Auth with client-side role guards on all admin views.
