Introduction & Context

E-Waste Crisis & The Circular Economy Imperative

Understanding the urgent shift from linear consumption to sustainable material recovery.

📈 The Escalating E-Waste Surge

Rapid technological cycles cause premature gadget disposal. The global economy generates 62 Million Metric Tonnes (Mt) of e-waste annually, accelerating to 82 Mt by 2030. India is the 3rd largest generator worldwide (>1.7 Mt/year).

♻️ Transition to Circular Economy

Traditional "Take-Make-Dispose" depletes finite ores. Circular recycling extracts high-purity copper, silver, and gold—yielding up to 800 times more gold per ton than raw natural earth ore!

Problem Analysis

The Problem Statement: Critical E-Waste Bottlenecks

Analyzing why conventional disposal systems fail at the consumer and municipal level.

⚠️

1. Toxic Contamination

ENVIRONMENTAL TOXICITY

  • • Lead (Pb): Solder & CRT glass cause severe brain and neurological damage.
  • • Mercury (Hg): Backlights leach into groundwater aquifers.
  • • Li-Ion Fires: Compactor trucks ignite violent chemical fires.
🏭

2. Informal Scrap Sector

UNREGULATED ACID BATHS

  • • >90% Share: E-waste ends up in crude backyard scrap yards.
  • • Open Acid Baths: Cyanide and acid leaching cause catastrophic health hazards.
  • • Low Recovery: Industrial refiners recover 98%; scrap yards lose >70% metals.
🚪

3. Consumer Friction

LOGISTICAL INERTIA

  • • Zero Visibility: Citizens don't know where authorized collection centers exist.
  • • No Logistics: No on-demand doorstep collection service for electronics.
  • • Data Privacy Fear: Users hoard obsolete gadgets fearing identity theft.
Proposed Solution

Database Engineering

Relational Database Schema & Row Level Security (RLS)

PostgreSQL 15 storage architecture enforcing database-level customer privacy and administrative clearance.

TABLE: pickups

  • • id: UUID (PK)
  • • user_id: UUID (FK)
  • • item_type: TEXT
  • • quantity: INTEGER (>=1)
  • • pickup_date: DATE
  • • address: TEXT
  • • status: TEXT ('pending'...)
  • • created_at: TIMESTAMPTZ

TABLE: recyclers

  • • id: UUID (PK)
  • • name: TEXT
  • • address: TEXT
  • • city: TEXT (Mandya/Blr)
  • • lat: FLOAT8
  • • lng: FLOAT8
  • • contact: TEXT
  • • accepts: TEXT[]

ROW LEVEL SECURITY

  • • User Isolation: auth.uid() = user_id guarantees citizens only view their own pickups.
  • • Public Locator: Unrestricted SELECT on recyclers for all visitors.
  • • Admin Clearance: Only accounts with profiles.role = 'admin' can update triage status.

Empirical Analytics

Mathematical Modeling of Environmental Impact

Scientific formulas translating recycled units into greenhouse gas offsets and mature tree equivalents.

📐 Core Mathematical Formulas

  • CO2e Prevented:
    CO2e (kg) = Diverted Units × 1.44 kg CO2e
    Averts carbon emissions from virgin open-pit ore mining.
  • Landfill Mass Diverted:
    Mass (kg) = Diverted Units × 2.10 kg
    Composite average weight across IT and home appliances.
  • Tree Absorption Equivalent:
    Trees = CO2e (kg) / 21.8 kg/tree/year
    USDA Forest Service baseline for urban mature trees.

Communication Architecture

Serverless Real-Time Email Notification Pipeline

Zero-backend transactional email dispatcher providing automated customer booking receipts and status alerts.

✉️ Serverless Architecture

  • • Zero Server Overhead: Browser SDK initiates HTTPS call; no dedicated Node.js mail daemon needed.
  • • Unified Credentials: Service ID service_np1cnlg & Template template_to9738m shared by both apps.
  • • Trigger 1 (Booking): Dispatches immediate confirmation receipt upon doorstep booking.
  • • Trigger 2 (Triage): Dispatches notification when admin marks item as 'Collected'.
  • • SLA: Direct inbox delivery in under 2.5 seconds.
